# ATTRIBUTION

Every binary asset shipped in OpenMaps (icons, images, fonts, sounds, styles) needs a row here in
the **same commit** that adds the file. Original work: author = "OpenMaps (original)". Licensed
third-party: name the author, source URL, and licence.

| File | Author | Source | Licence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Material Icons font (bundled via `uses-material-design: true`) | Google / Flutter | https://github.com/google/material-design-icons | Apache-2.0 | Icon glyphs referenced through `Icons.*`. Permissively licensed; not Google Maps iconography. |
| Phosphor Icons font (bundled via `phosphor_icons: ^3.0.1`) | Phosphor Icons | https://github.com/phosphor-icons/phosphor-icons | MIT | Payment-method glyphs on the place sheet (`hand-coins`, `currency-btc`, `credit-card`, …). |
| `test/features/goldens/*.png` | OpenMaps (original) | generated by `flutter test --update-goldens` | n/a | Test fixtures only — never shipped in the app. |

Rules (see `AGENTS.md` §Assets): nothing from Google Maps or any proprietary product; original,
public domain/CC0, OSI-open-source, or CC-with-redistribution only. Screenshots under
`plan/maps/screenshots/` are observation material and are **never** assets.
